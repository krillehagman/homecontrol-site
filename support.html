Common questions

The app says it can't find a home. Home Control shows the homes you have in Apple's Home app. Set one up there first, and allow Home Control to use Home data (Settings › Privacy & Security › HomeKit). Until then you can look around an example home from the tour.

A device shows as connected but doesn't answer. That usually means the home hub (an Apple TV or HomePod) has lost touch with the device's bridge. Try choosing another home hub in the Home app, or restart the bridge.

Do I need a Mac? No. A Mac at home running Home Control can run rules and read your network around the clock, but everything else works on its own.

Weather, network and electricity prices are empty. They need a Netatmo weather station, a UniFi router, or a price area chosen in Settings. Without them, the rest of the app works as usual.
